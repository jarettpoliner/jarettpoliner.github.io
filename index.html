Jarett Poliner

Postdoctoral Research Associate · Los Alamos National Laboratory

I'm a postdoctoral research associate at Los Alamos National Laboratory, working on machine learning for physical systems. I'm interested in generative modeling, geometric deep learning, learned simulation, and reconstructing physical worlds from sparse observations.

I received my PhD in Civil Engineering and Engineering Mechanics from Columbia University in 2025, where I worked with Prof. WaiChing Sun on generative modeling for solid mechanics.

Research

Generative modeling of complex materials

Learning particle morphology, spatial arrangement, and topology from limited three-dimensional experimental data to generate representative material structures for downstream physics simulations.

Learning physical dynamics from sparse observations

Developing data-driven models that combine incomplete sensor measurements, contextual information, and physical structure for state estimation and forecasting, including urban traffic systems.

Physics-aware generative models

Using probabilistic and generative learning to construct constitutive descriptions and physical representations from sparse data while respecting mechanical constraints.
